## 2. Australian EV Market Landscape (2024-2026)

### 2.1 Market Size & EV Adoption

| Metric | Value | Context |
|--------|-------|---------|
| **Total new car sales (2025)** | ~1.2M units/year | Flat market |
| **EV share of new car sales** | ~8-12% (2025) | Growing but slow |
| **EV penetration** | Lowest among developed nations | Only ~8% vs. Norway 95%, UK 22% |
| **Growth rate** | 40-50% YoY | Accelerating |

**Why Australia's EV Adoption is Low (Contrast with Other Markets):**

| Factor | Impact |
|--------|--------|
| **No federal EV purchase incentive** | Australia has NO national EV subsidy (unlike UK, France, Germany) |
| **Large distances** | Range anxiety more acute — cities are far apart |
| **Charging infrastructure** | Limited outside major cities |
| **Luxury car tax** | EVs still attract LCT (Luxury Car Tax) on high-end models |
| **State incentives vary** | ACT has stamp duty exemption; NSW has rebates; most states have nothing |

### 2.2 Australian EV Incentives by State

| State | Incentive | Status |
|-------|---------|--------|

| **Queensland** | No purchase incentive | Only registration discounts |

**Context:** The absence of a federal EV incentive means Australia lags behind comparable nations.

## 4. Distance & Geography Challenges in Australia

### 4.1 Australia's Unique EV Challenges

| Challenge | Impact on EV Buyers |
|-----------|--------------------|
| **Long distances** | Range anxiety more acute (no city-to-city ICE equivalent) |
| **Charging gaps** | Major routes (Sydney-Melbourne) have fast chargers but many routes don't |
| **Sparse population** | Regional EV buyers face infrastructure challenges |
| **Outback travel** | No EV can handle outback routes (not an issue for urban buyers) |

## 5. Government Policy & Why Australia Lags in EVs

### 5.1 Why Australia Has No Federal EV Incentive

- Australia is a major fossil fuel exporter (coal, gas, iron ore)
- No federal government has prioritized EV incentives
- Political complexity around "electric vehicles = climate policy"
- States have acted independently (ACT, NSW took initiative)

### 5.2 Luxury Car Tax (LCT) on EVs

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **LCT threshold** | ~$89k for fuel-efficient vehicles |
| **LCT rate** | 33% on amount over threshold |
| **Impact** | EVs over $89k pay LCT (Tesla Model Y perf., Polestar 2, etc.) |
| **Comparison** | Norway exempts EVs from all VAT/taxes |

**Global Lesson:** Tax policy significantly affects EV adoption. Removing LCT could boost premium EV sales.

### 5.3 Future Policy Outlook (2025-2026)

- New federal government (as of 2025) has signaled EV support
- Phase-out of fuel efficiency standards (previously none existed)
- New vehicle efficiency standards being considered
- Impact: Would force OEMs to bring more EVs to Australia

## 6. Gaps & Unmet Needs (Australian First-Time EV Buyers)

### 6.1 No Real-World Range Data
- No Australian platform shows real-world range (EPA is US-centric, not Australian context)
- Australian buyers need "real range in Australian summer/winter" data
- Hot climate (Queensland, NT) affects battery differently than cold climates

**Opportunity:** "Australian Real-World EV Range Database" — real owner data on range in Australian conditions.

### 6.2 Charging Infrastructure Transparency
- Which apartments/homes can install home charging?
- What are the public charging options near my home/work?
- How much does home charging installation cost?

**Opportunity:** "Charging Readiness Assessment" for Australian homes — particularly apartment dwellers.

### 6.3 EV vs. ICE True Cost Comparison (Australian Context)
- Electricity prices in Australia (high residential electricity costs)
- Petrol prices (relatively low, but fluctuating)
- Road tax (Victoria has EV tax, others don't)
- Registration costs vary by state

**Opportunity:** "Australian EV TCO Calculator" — true cost of EV vs. ICE over 3-5 years in Australian context.

### 6.4 Grey Import Considerations
- Some Australians consider importing EVs from Japan, UK, or US
- No platform helps with import process, costs, compliance

**Opportunity:** "EV Import Guide" for Australia — what's the real cost of importing a Tesla from UK vs. buying locally?

### 6.5 Multi-Brand EV Configurator
- Tesla has great configurator (but only Tesla)
- No platform lets you configure + compare across BYD, MG, Polestar, Kia, Hyundai

**Opportunity:** "Multi-Brand EV Configurator" for Australia — compare all EVs in one place.

## 7. Financing & Purchase (Australia)

### 7.1 How Australians Finance Car Purchases

| Method | Penetration | Notes |
|--------|------------|-------|
| **Car Loan** | Most common | 6-9% interest rates |
| **Finance/Lease through dealer** | Common | Dealer-arranged financing |
| **Personal Loan** | Less common | Unsecured, higher rate |
| **Cash Purchase** | Significant | Major purchase, many pay cash |
| **Novated Lease** | Common (salary packaging) | Pre-tax dollars for car financing |

### 7.2 EV-Specific Financing Challenges

- Some banks still treat EVs as "higher risk" (new technology)
- Interest rates can be slightly higher for EVs at some lenders
- Gap: No platform shows EV-specific financing deals

**Opportunity:** "EV Financing Comparator" — show financing deals from all major Australian lenders for EVs.

### 7.3 Trade-in Process in Australia

- Major platforms (CarsGuide, Drive) have trade-in tools
- Dealers offer trade-ins
- Online valuations available
- Gap: No platform shows expected EV depreciation (how much will your EV be worth in 3 years?)

**Opportunity:** "EV Depreciation Calculator" — estimate future value based on battery health + market trends.
