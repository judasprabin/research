## 4. HouseSmart SWOT

### Strengths
- Only AU app combining receipt + grocery list + nutrition + bank sync
- Zero-effort nutrition (from purchases, not meal logging)
- Cross-store price comparison (structurally unavailable from supermarkets)
- AU-first — Basiq, CDR, ADG, AU supermarket formats

### Weaknesses
- No brand recognition
- Receipt scanning habit is hard to form
- Small team vs well-funded incumbents
- Price comparison needs critical mass of scan data

### Opportunities
- Open Banking in AU maturing rapidly
- January health resolution market (biggest signup spike)
- FMCG brands want AU household purchase data
- White-label to health insurers (Bupa, Medibank wellness programs)

### Threats
- Privacy concerns about receipt + bank access
- ALDI receipt accuracy issues (minimal itemisation)
- Basiq pricing at scale
- Supermarket building competing features
